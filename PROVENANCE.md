# Project Provenance

## Public project origin

**Project:** THE FIRST SECOND  
**Repository:** `vcheeko/the-first-second`  
**Public repository established:** 22 August 2026  
**Initial project originator:** Miha Tavčar (`vcheeko`)

THE FIRST SECOND began as a simulation-game concept exploring whether a player can ever prove that the reality they inhabit is not itself a simulation.

The initial public concept includes the following connected design directions:

- the first second after the Big Bang as a foundational event;
- seconds as a core relationship between a player and local reality rather than a conventional health bar;
- existence that may be physical, informational, energetic, light-based, conscious, synthetic, or non-classical depending on the simulation;
- nested simulations and transitions between simulation layers;
- a special entity, The First Second, associated with cross-simulation memory and movement;
- the possibility that approaching zero seconds changes access to reality rather than simply causing game over.

The canonical public description is maintained in [`CONCEPT.md`](CONCEPT.md).
