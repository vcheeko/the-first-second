# Contributing to THE FIRST SECOND

THE FIRST SECOND is currently a public concept / pre-prototype project, but it is **not open source** and does not currently accept unsolicited code, artwork, music, narrative, game-design material, or other creative contributions.

## Before contributing

Please do not submit substantive creative or technical material unless the project owner has explicitly invited the contribution and the rights and permissions for that contribution have been agreed in writing.

This is intended to keep authorship, ownership, licensing and future commercial use clear while the project is still being defined.

## Issues and discussion

Ideas, questions and general feedback may be discussed publicly, but posting an idea or suggestion does not by itself create an obligation for the project to use it, compensate for it, or treat it as confidential.

Do not post confidential, proprietary, third-party, or restricted material.

## Future contribution model

Before outside development begins, the project intends to establish a documented contributor process covering at least:

- identity of the contributor;
- description of the contribution;
- confirmation that the contributor has the right to provide it;
- ownership or licence terms;
- permission for modification, distribution and commercial use;
- treatment of third-party and AI-assisted material.

Until that process exists, substantive unsolicited contributions should not be submitted.
